Bolognese

from Mika

INGREDIENTS:

  • 1 large brown onion
  • 2 cloves of garlic
  • 1 can of chopped tomatoes
  • 1 bottle of passata
  • 1 little plastic thing (the technical term) of tomato paste
  • 500 gram beef mince
  • basil
  • oregano
  • spaghetti

METHOD:

  1. Finely chop the onion, and put it in a pan with some olive oil over medium heat.
  2. Fry this gently until the onion turns golden brown, don't burn it, and take your time with this step it's worth it for the flavour.
  3. Add the garlic (I use a press, but you can chop it up finely too if you prefer).
  4. Add about a teaspoon of the basil and oregano (or just eyeball it like I do)
  5. Fry for another minute or two but stir constantly and dont let the garlic burn.
  6. Add the mince meat, turn up the heat a little so that the meat actually browns properly.
  7. Once the meat is cooked, add puree, chopped tomatoes, and paste.
  8. Turn the heat down a bit and reduce the sauce until its nice and thick. After a few minutes taste to check if you need more basil or oregano since this is the last point you can add them.
  9. Season to taste with salt and pepper, but don't add the other spices again this late.
  10. Serve with your preferred pasta and parmesan (the parmesan is not optional).
